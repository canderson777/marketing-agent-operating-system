# Content Pillars

Proposed 2026-07-07 from shipped product features (repo README + store-listing.md). These are grounded in what the app actually does, but the owner should confirm or reorder them.

## Pillar 1
- topic: Fridge-to-recipe demos - show real ingredients going in, a full recipe coming out in seconds.
- why it matters: It demos the core "what's in my fridge?" promise, the app's strongest hook.

## Pillar 2
- topic: Weekly meal planning and the auto grocery list.
- why it matters: Shows the plan-to-shopping-list workflow that separates Prep2Eat from plain recipe generators.

## Pillar 3
- topic: Save recipes from anywhere - URL and social video (TikTok/Instagram/YouTube) import.
- why it matters: Meets people where recipe discovery already happens; import feature is shipped/in progress.

## Pillar 4
- topic: Build-in-public app updates (mascot reveals, Play Store journey, feature ships).
- why it matters: Prep2Eat's stated portfolio role is proving the marketing system "can support a product/app, not just a service business", and build content feeds the founder's build-in-public content too.
