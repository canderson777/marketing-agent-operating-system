# Audience

Sources: Prep2Eat repo `README.md` and `docs/store-listing.md`. Enriched 2026-07-07.

## Primary audience
- Home cooks organizing their favorite recipes and food enthusiasts building meal plans (README: "Whether you're a home cook looking to organize your favorite recipes or a food enthusiast wanting to create meal plans").
- Age 13+ per store listing.
- Copy targets busy people who want fast answers: "Got chicken, broccoli, and rice? Prep2Eat writes a recipe in seconds."

## Secondary audience
- People saving recipes from TikTok/Instagram/YouTube videos (social import is a shipped/in-progress feature; README example URLs are heavy on short-form video recipes).
- NEEDS OWNER INPUT: any deliberate secondary segment (families? fitness/health eaters? budget shoppers?). The Dr. Hyman health-recipe examples in the README hint at health-conscious cooks, but that is not stated as a target.

## Pain points
- Deciding what to cook with what is already in the fridge (store-listing.md).
- Recipes scattered across sites, screenshots, and saved videos.
- Making a shopping list from a meal plan by hand.

## Desired outcomes
- A week of meals planned in minutes, grocery list auto-done, recipe library in one place.

## Objections
- Privacy/ads fatigue - answered directly in store copy: no ads, no tracking, no data selling.
- "Another subscription" - answered: core features free, no paywalls.
- NEEDS OWNER INPUT: other objections heard from real testers.

## Buying triggers
- (Adoption triggers - app is free): weekly meal-prep moment, seeing a recipe video they want to keep, grocery-planning day.
- NEEDS OWNER INPUT: confirm from early user behavior once live.

## Notes
- No real-user data yet (app in testing). Everything above is from product copy, not observed users.
