# Business Overview

Sources: `the Prep2Eat app README`, `docs/store-listing.md` (Play Store draft). Enriched 2026-07-07 from repo docs only - no invented facts.

## Brand Name
Prep2Eat

## What this brand/business is
- AI-powered recipe generator, meal planner, and smart grocery list in one app (store-listing.md short description).
- "Prep2Eat turns 'what's in my fridge?' into a weekly meal plan in under a minute" - type ingredients or a craving, the AI generates a complete recipe with ingredients, instructions, prep time, and serving notes (store-listing.md full description).
- Also extracts recipes from any URL, including social video recipes - "Transform any recipe URL into a personalized cooking experience" (README).
- Tagline used on store feature graphic: "AI recipes. Real plans."

## What it sells
- The app is free: "No ads. No tracking pixels. No selling your data." and "no paywalls on core features" (store-listing.md).
- NEEDS OWNER INPUT: monetization plan (premium tier? sponsorship? stay free as portfolio proof?). Nothing in the repo states one.

## Core problem it solves
- "What's in my fridge?" paralysis - turns available ingredients into a recipe in seconds (store-listing.md).
- Recipe chaos - saves and organizes recipes from anywhere into one library with duplicate protection (README).
- Meal-plan-to-shopping friction - the weekly plan auto-generates a checkable, grouped grocery list (store-listing.md).

## Main transformation / outcome
- From scattered recipes and daily "what's for dinner" stress to a planned week of meals with the shopping list already done.
