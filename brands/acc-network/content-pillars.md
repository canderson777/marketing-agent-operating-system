# Content Pillars

## Pillar 1
- topic: plain-English AI news
- why it matters: this is the core promise of the brand

## Pillar 2
- topic: AI model, tool, and product explainers
- why it matters: helps readers understand launches without hype overload

## Pillar 3
- topic: curated opportunities
- why it matters: jobs, courses, events, and useful links make the brand practical

## Pillar 4
- topic: blog deep dives and educational breakdowns
- why it matters: builds SEO, trust, and longer-form value beyond quick news

## Notes
- keep content helpful for both curious beginners and practical builders
- avoid drifting too far into generic web3 or broad tech noise unless clearly relevant
