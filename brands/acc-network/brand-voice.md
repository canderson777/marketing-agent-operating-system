# Brand Voice

## Voice summary
- plain English
- grounded
- useful
- clear
- beginner-friendly

## Tone traits
- explains without showing off
- calm, not breathless
- motivating but not hype-heavy
- structured and readable
- accessible to beginners while still useful to builders

## Words / phrases to use
- plain English
- what this means
- why it matters
- quick answer
- beginner-friendly
- useful update
- worth watching
- here is the real takeaway

## Words / phrases to avoid
- overhyped launch language
- heavy research-lab jargon without explanation
- corporate fluff
- aggressive clickbait
- em dashes in newsletter/blog copy where the workflow forbids them

## Example style notes
- explain technical topics simply
- translate launches into practical meaning
- use short paragraphs
- prefer clarity over sounding smart
- keep newsletter headlines grounded and motivating

## CTA style
- subscribe for daily AI signal
- read the full breakdown
- explore the blog
- work with us

## Reference notes
The newsletter workflow specifically calls for:
- plain English
- motivating but grounded copy
- beginner-friendly framing
- no em dashes
- no corporate voice
