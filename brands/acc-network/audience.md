# Audience

## Primary audience
- people curious about AI
- beginners trying to understand new tools and model releases
- busy professionals who want simple AI updates
- early builders and developers who want useful summaries without noise

## Secondary audience
- newsletter readers who want curated AI stories
- learners looking for courses, jobs, events, and explainers
- business owners who want to stay current without living on X all day

## Pain points
- AI news is too noisy
- too much jargon
- hard to tell what matters
- not enough plain-English explanation
- easy to feel behind

## Desired outcomes
- understand AI faster
- find useful stories and tools
- keep up without overwhelm
- learn practical implications of model and product releases
- discover learning, job, and event opportunities

## Objections
- "this will probably just be another hype newsletter"
- "will this actually explain things simply?"
- "is this for beginners or only devs?"

## Buying triggers
- plain-English explanations
- consistent quality and curation
- trustworthy filtering of what matters
- useful structure across newsletter, blog, and site content

## Notes
- strongest audience positioning is beginner-friendly without sounding dumbed down
- good fit for readers who want calm signal over constant hype
