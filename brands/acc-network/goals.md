## KPIs / success metrics
- subscriber growth
- open rates and click rates
- site traffic
- consistency of newsletter output
- blog publishing frequency
- sponsor/partner interest

Baseline (MailerLite, 2026-07-08): 11 active subscribers; lifetime open rate 19.12%; click rate 1.47%; last send 2026-05-21. Measure all growth against these numbers. At 11 subscribers, subscriber acquisition is the KPI that matters - open-rate movements are statistical noise until the list is bigger (1 open = 9 points).
