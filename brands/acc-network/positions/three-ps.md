# Three P's — ACC Network

> Positioning groundwork. Source: `business-overview.md`, `audience.md`
> (verified from site repo `the ACC Network site repo` 2026-07-07).

## Person
**Who, specifically, feels the pain?**
- **Archetype / role:** Busy people curious about AI — early builders, devs,
  business owners — who want to stay on top of AI without getting buried.
- What they care about: understanding what matters in AI, plainly; finding useful
  stories and tools; not falling behind.
- Where they hang out: email (newsletter), X (@ACC_Official1), LinkedIn, the
  site/blog.

## Pain
- Core problem: AI moves fast and the space is noisy — hard to tell what matters.
- What it costs them: overwhelm, jargon fatigue, fear of falling behind, wasted
  time on hype.
- How they feel: "another hype newsletter will probably not explain things
  simply."
- Tried already: other news feeds that are noisy or too technical.

## Promise
- The offer: **Your Daily AI Signal, in Plain English** — a daily 5-minute brief
  curating model releases, vetted tools, robotics, jobs, X Spaces.
- Outcome: readers stay informed, not overwhelmed; calm, clear signal.
- Why credible: live site (accnetwork.xyz), MailerLite daily workflow, plain-
  English beginner-friendly voice, real founder ("Taxi driver by day, AI builder
  by night").
- Differentiator: beginner-friendly without sounding dumbed down; calm signal
  over constant hype.

## Positioning Statement (draft)
> For busy non-tech leaders overwhelmed by AI noise, **ACC Network** is the
> daily plain-English AI brief that tells you what matters and keeps you from
> falling behind.
