# Scholarship Dashboard Audience

## Primary audience

New York high school seniors and college freshmen interested in:

- technology
- computer science
- engineering
- IT
- cybersecurity
- AI
- data science
- STEM-related degrees

## Secondary audience

- parents helping students organize scholarship applications
- school counselors
- college access nonprofits
- STEM programs

## Audience pain points

- scholarship search is messy and repetitive
- eligibility rules are hard to compare
- deadlines get missed
- students do not know which awards are worth prioritizing
- application materials are scattered
- scholarship sites feel generic and overloaded

## Messaging angle

Make scholarship search feel less like a random Google hunt and more like a prioritized checklist.
