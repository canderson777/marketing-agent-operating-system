# Three P's — Scholarship Dashboard

> Positioning groundwork. Source: `business-overview.md`, `audience.md`
> (project folder `the Scholarship Dashboard project folder`).

## Person
- **Archetype / role:** New York high school seniors and college freshmen
  pursuing tech/STEM degrees (CS, engineering, IT, cybersecurity, AI, data
  science).
- What they care about: not missing scholarship money; knowing which awards are
  worth their time; staying organized.
- Where they hang out: Google searching scholarships, school counselors, college
  access programs.

## Pain
- Core problem: scholarship search is messy and repetitive.
- Eligibility rules are hard to compare; deadlines get missed; students don't
  know which awards to prioritize; application materials are scattered;
  scholarship sites feel generic and overloaded.
- How they feel about it: overwhelmed, at risk of missing money.

## Promise
- The offer: an MVP web app to find, prioritize, track, and prepare applications
  for tech/STEM scholarships — dates/materials/eligibility in one place.
- Outcome: make scholarship search feel less like a random Google hunt, more like
  a prioritized checklist.
- Why credible: [PROOF / TRACK RECORD — `NEEDS OWNER INPUT`]; product is
  early (still building).
- Constraint (honest): start narrow — NY + tech/STEM + HS seniors/college
  freshmen; broaden later (women-only, other subjects/degrees).

## Positioning Statement (draft)
> For NY students chasing tech and STEM scholarships, **Scholarship Dashboard**
> is the organizer that turns a messy random search into a prioritized checklist —
> so they don't miss the money they're eligible for.
