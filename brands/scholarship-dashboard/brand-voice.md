# Scholarship Dashboard Brand Voice

## Tone

Clear, practical, helpful, student-friendly.

## Style

- simple language
- no hype
- no fearmongering
- no fake inspiration
- specific deadlines and next steps
- organized checklist energy

## Avoid

- sounding like a generic scholarship blog
- overpromising guaranteed money
- implying AI should fabricate student stories
- broad national positioning before the NY/STEM MVP is useful
