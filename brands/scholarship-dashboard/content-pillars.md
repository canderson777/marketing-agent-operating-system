# Scholarship Dashboard Content Pillars

## 1. Scholarship discovery

Help students find relevant tech/STEM scholarships without wasting hours.

## 2. Prioritization

Teach students how to rank scholarships by fit, deadline, effort, and award value.

## 3. Application organization

Checklists, timelines, document prep, recommendation planning, and deadline reminders.

## 4. NY/STEM opportunities

Focused scholarship updates for New York students pursuing tech/STEM paths.

## 5. Honest AI support

Use AI to organize, brainstorm, revise, and clarify — not fabricate achievements or essays.
