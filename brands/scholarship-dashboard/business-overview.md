# Scholarship Dashboard Business Overview

## What it is

Scholarship Dashboard is an MVP web app helping New York high school seniors and college freshmen find, prioritize, track, and prepare applications for tech/STEM scholarships.

## Current stage

Still building. The product should stay narrow and useful before expanding.

## Core value

Help students quickly answer:

- Which scholarships should I apply to next?
- Am I eligible?
- What do I need to submit?
- What deadline matters first?
- How do I stay organized without missing money?

## Product constraint

Start with NY + tech/STEM + high school seniors/college freshmen. Preserve room for future states, women-only awards, other subjects, and other degrees, but do not lead with broad scope yet.
