# Audience

Updated 2026-07-10 from the operator's sales materials and campaign decision. The broader audience remains local businesses driven by calls and bookings; the initial validation campaign is intentionally narrower.

## Primary audience
Owner-operated local businesses whose revenue comes from **calls and bookings** — the audit checklist's own examples are service businesses ("plumber in Dallas", appointment-based, walk-ins).

**Initial validation beachhead: independent plumbing companies in the Bronx, NYC, with legitimate reviews but weak Google/Maps visibility.** This applies to the first 10 audits. Review replies, calls, and objections after those audits before continuing, changing the message, or testing another vertical.

## Secondary audience
Any local business that finds the ebook/capture page organically. Don't build for them yet.

## Pain points (from the discovery script — these are the questions the script asks, so they're the pains the business expects)
- "Should be getting more calls than we are."
- Competitors show up above them on Google / in the Map Pack.
- Did good work for years but online presence doesn't reflect it.
- Missed calls, missed bookings, missed reviews.
- Burned before: "worked with a marketing/SEO company and it went nowhere."

## Desired outcomes
More calls and booked jobs from people already searching. Not "brand awareness," not traffic.

## Objections (anticipated by the sales scripts)
- "Tried an agency before, got nothing" → counter: personal video audit before any money changes hands; founder does the work, no handoffs.
- "Too expensive" → counter: cost-of-inaction framing from the script ("what happens if nothing changes for 6–12 months?"); Founding Partner pricing.
- "No time" → counter: audit is delivered as a video they watch whenever; the service is done-for-you.

## Buying triggers
- Just saw a competitor outrank them or run ads on their name.
- Slow month / phone stopped ringing.
- Just opened, rebranded, or moved location (NAP inconsistency).
- Received the video audit and saw themselves NOT appear in a live Map Pack search — this moment is the whole strategy.

## Notes
- Write copy to one person: the owner who answers their own phone.
- Never say "broken" — frame as missed opportunity (audit checklist mindset rule).
