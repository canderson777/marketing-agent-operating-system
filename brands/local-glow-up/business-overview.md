## Brand Name
Local Glow Up — localglowup.com (live on Vercel, custom domain + SSL verified).

## What this brand/business is
A local-visibility service for local businesses. Site tagline: **"Get Found on Google, Maps & AI Search."** Meta description: "Local Glow Up helps local businesses show up on Google, Maps, and AI tools like ChatGPT. Free visibility audit — find out exactly where you're invisible online."

## What it sells
Approved offer ladder, reflected in the current homepage branch:
- **Free Visibility Audit** — no-cost personalized video diagnosis with exactly three prioritized fixes
- **Visibility Fix Sprint** — $499 one-time
- **Website Foundation** — $1,299 setup + $99/mo
- **Local Growth System** — $1,999 setup + $399/mo
- **Growth Partner** — from $799/mo after an audit

Entry offer: **Free Visibility Audit** — a personally recorded Loom video (screen + face cam) covering Google Business Profile, website speed/UX, and search visibility, ending with exactly 3 prioritized fixes. Full recording checklist exists (`LocalGlowUp_Audit_Checklist.docx`).

Launch offer: **Founding Partner Offer** — up to three founding clients can join the Local Growth System at $1,499 setup + $299/mo for the first six months while the service is documented and refined, in exchange for permission to document the work, prompt feedback, and an honest testimonial if the engagement earns one.

## Core problem it solves
Local businesses do good work but are invisible where customers actually search — Google Map Pack, "near me" searches, and increasingly AI tools (ChatGPT recommendations). That gap costs them calls, bookings, and reviews.

## Main transformation / outcome
More calls and bookings from local search. Framed on-site as "Simple Pricing. Clear Scope." and via the three strategy levers in the growth model: outcome-led messaging, agentic SEO (AI search visibility), and 24-hour speed-to-lead.
