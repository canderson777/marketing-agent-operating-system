## Offer ladder (as it exists today)

1. **Free ebook** — "Local Glow Up Visibility Guide" ("Why Local Businesses Are Invisible Online"). Delivered via capture page → MailerLite. Purpose: warm cold traffic. CTA inside → free audit.
2. **Free Visibility Audit** (entry offer, THE conversion engine) — personally recorded Loom video: Google Business Profile check, live Map Pack search, PageSpeed score on screen, exactly 3 prioritized fixes. Followed by the 3-email sequence (Day 0 deliver / Day 2 clarify / Day 5 soft close) → Calendly call.
3. **Visibility Fix Sprint** — $499 one-time. Up to three agreed fixes from the audit, a before/after checklist, and one handoff call.
4. **Website Foundation** — $1,299 setup + $99/mo. Up to five responsive pages, the primary contact/call/booking path, hosting/SSL, basic local SEO, and bounded updates.
5. **Local Growth System** — $1,999 setup + $399/mo. Website foundation plus Google Business Profile optimization, local/AI-assisted discovery checks, review-request workflow, missed-call text-back setup, and one defined monthly content or optimization deliverable.
6. **Growth Partner** — from $799/mo after an audit. Custom ongoing content, visibility optimization, reporting, and strategy access.

## Launch accelerator
**Founding Partner Offer** — up to three founding clients can join the Local Growth System at $1,499 setup + $299/mo for the first six months while the service is documented and refined. The exchange is permission to document the work, prompt feedback, and an honest testimonial if the engagement earns one.

## Strongest offer/CTA (strategy call, 2026-07-07)
**The Free Visibility Audit is the primary CTA everywhere** — outreach, posts, capture page, bio links. Reasons:
- Zero case studies exist; the audit manufactures trust without proof.
- The complete fulfillment system already exists (checklist, email sequence, call script) — no new assets needed.
- It produces *conversations*, which is the stated goal, and each audit doubles as content raw material.
- The Founding Partner Offer is the close on the call, not the public hook.
