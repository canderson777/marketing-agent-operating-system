# Content Pillars

Updated 2026-07-07. Four pillars, all serviceable today by one person with the existing assets. Every post CTA points to the Free Visibility Audit.

## Pillar 1 — Visibility teardowns (the workhorse)
- topic: Anonymous, specific breakdowns of real local-business visibility gaps — Map Pack absence, wrong GBP category, mobile PageSpeed under 40, unanswered reviews, NAP mismatches. Screenshots or plain description; business anonymized unless they gave permission.
- why: Directly demonstrates the audit. Each outreach audit produces 1–2 of these for free. Specific beats clever.

## Pillar 2 — AI search visibility (the wedge)
- topic: "Does ChatGPT recommend your business?" — how AI tools pick local recommendations, why review text/GBP data feeds them, live examples of asking AI for "best [service] in [city]".
- why: The site already leads with it ("Google, Maps & AI Search") and almost no local competitor covers it. Freshest angle in the deck.

## Pillar 3 — DIY fixes that build trust
- topic: Practical fixes an owner can do themselves in under 30 minutes (fill business hours, answer GBP Q&A, make phone number tap-to-call, the 5-second homepage test). Straight from the ebook and audit checklist.
- why: Giving away real fixes is the trust engine for a no-proof brand. Filters for owners who value the work but want it done for them.

## Pillar 4 — Build-in-public / founding clients
- topic: The honest journey of taking on 3 founding clients — what an audit revealed, what got fixed, before/after numbers (speed scores, Map Pack position, calls) as they land.
- why: Converts the biggest weakness (no case studies) into content. Becomes Pillar-1 proof material over time.

## Notes
- Ratio guide: 2 teardowns : 1 AI-search : 1 DIY : 1 build-in-public per week.
- No posts about marketing theory, tools-for-marketers, or agency life — audience is business owners, not marketers.
