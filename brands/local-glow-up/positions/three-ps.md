# Three P's — Local Glow Up

> Positioning groundwork. Source: `business-overview.md`, `audience.md`,
> `offers.md` (verified from `the Local Glow Up project folder` 2026-07-07).

## Person
**Who, specifically, feels the pain?**
- **Archetype / role:** Owner-operated local business owners whose revenue
  comes from calls and bookings (service businesses — plumbers, appointment
  based, walk-ins). Initial beachhead: independent plumbing companies in the
  Bronx, NYC.
- Demographics: small service-business owners, `NEEDS OWNER INPUT` for exact
  range.
- What they care about: calls and booked jobs, not "brand awareness" or traffic.
- Where they hang out: Google search, Map Pack, "near me" searches, ChatGPT
  recommendations.

## Pain
- Core problem: local businesses do good work but are invisible where customers
  actually search (Google Map Pack, near me, AI tools) — losing calls, bookings,
  and reviews.
- What it costs them: missed calls, missed bookings, missed reviews, lost
  ground to competitors showing up above them.
- How they feel about it: "Should be getting more calls than we are." Burned by
  agencies before.
- What they've tried: marketing/SEO companies that "went nowhere."

## Promise
- The offer: Local visibility service that gets them found on Google, Maps, and
  AI search. Entry: **Free Visibility Audit** (personal Loom video, 3 fixes).
- The outcome: more calls and booked jobs from people already searching.
- Why credible: personal video audit before money; founder does the work, no
  handoffs; live site + verified infrastructure.
- Differentiators: AI-search angle ("does ChatGPT recommend you?"), personal
  video audit, anti-agency positioning.

## Positioning Statement (draft)
> For local service businesses losing calls to invisible search presence,
> **Local Glow Up** is the visibility service that gets you found on Google,
> Maps, and AI — with a free personal audit that shows exactly where you're
> invisible.
