# Brand Voice

Updated 2026-07-07. Voice is NOT undefined — it's demonstrated consistently across the email sequence, sales scripts, audit checklist, and site copy. This file codifies what those materials already do.

## Voice summary
Calm, direct, specific, low-pressure. A guide who has looked at *your* business, not a closer working a list. Confident enough to say "if it's not a fit, I'll tell you that too."

## Tone traits
- Plain sentences. Short paragraphs. No exclamation-mark energy.
- Specific over general: name the search term, the score, the competitor position.
- Selective, not needy: silence after 3 touches = "not interested (for now). That's fine."
- Frames problems as **missed opportunity, never failure** ("Never say 'broken'" — audit checklist).
- Slightly cleaner/more professional than the operator's personal brand voice.

## Words / phrases to use (pulled from existing materials)
- "Here's exactly what I found."
- "Where customers are likely missing you."
- "If this were my business, I'd start with…"
- "No rush — I just didn't want this to get buried in your inbox."
- "Either is fine — I just don't want you stuck in the middle."
- "Fair?"
- Missed calls / missed bookings / invisible / show up / get found.

## Words / phrases to avoid (explicit rules from the email sequence coach notes)
- Urgency theater: "spots are filling up", "act now", countdown timers.
- Discounts as chasing. No discounts in follow-up.
- "Just checking in."
- Agency jargon: synergy, full-funnel, holistic, cutting-edge, revolutionize.
- Hype adjectives: game-changing, skyrocket, 10x, crushing it.
- More than 3 follow-ups.

## Example on-voice copy (canonical references)
- Email 1 of the sequence: "This isn't a sales pitch — just a clear look at what's going on and what I'd focus on if this were my business."
- Capture page: "A simple, no-BS guide. No spam. No sales pitch."
- Site: "No fluff. No handoffs. Just results."

## CTA style
Soft, optional, concrete. "If any of this resonates and you want to talk options, I've included a Calendly link. No pressure — happy to just answer questions too." Always one CTA, always the audit or the call, never both in one message.
