# Content Rules

Use these rules for blogs, social posts, emails, scripts, ads, landing pages, and outreach.

## Core Rules

- Start with the audience's problem, desire, or context.
- Make the offer and CTA clear.
- Use the brand voice.
- Match the channel format.
- Use proof when available.
- Do not invent claims.
- Keep every asset tied to a measurable goal.

## Content Pillars

Most profiles should have three to five pillars:

- Education: teach useful ideas.
- Proof: show results, examples, or process.
- Authority: explain the point of view.
- Trust: show personality, values, and behind-the-scenes.
- Conversion: invite action.

## Hook Standards

A strong hook should do at least one:

- Name a specific problem.
- Promise a useful outcome.
- Challenge a common assumption.
- Show a surprising detail.
- Create urgency without fake pressure.
- Make the audience feel seen.

## CTA Standards

Every asset should have one clear CTA:

- Book a call.
- Join the list.
- Download the guide.
- Reply with a word.
- Try the product.
- Read the post.
- Watch the demo.
- Share with a friend.

## Repurposing Rules

When repurposing:

- Keep the core message.
- Change the format for the channel.
- Do not paste the same copy everywhere.
- Preserve claims and compliance.
- Adjust length, tone, and CTA.

## Content Quality Checklist

- Specific audience.
- Specific problem.
- Specific value.
- Clear CTA.
- Channel-appropriate format.
- No unsupported claims.
- No generic filler.
- Next action included.
