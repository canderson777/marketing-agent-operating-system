# Compliance Rules

These are general marketing safety rules. They are not legal advice. When the campaign involves regulated categories, get professional review before launch.

## Always Required

- Do not invent facts, testimonials, results, revenue, user counts, awards, or endorsements.
- Do not guarantee outcomes unless the client has approved legal language.
- Label sponsored, affiliate, paid, or gifted relationships.
- Keep health, finance, legal, and earnings claims conservative and proof-based.
- Use clear opt-out language for email and outreach when required.
- Respect platform policies for ads and social posts.

## Claims

Use only:

- Verified facts from the profile or brief.
- Clearly labeled estimates.
- Soft claims based on logic, not guarantees.
- Testimonials or case studies provided by the client.

Avoid:

- Guaranteed income.
- Guaranteed weight loss or health outcomes.
- Guaranteed rankings.
- Guaranteed ad performance.
- "Risk-free" unless truly accurate.
- "Everyone" or "no one" claims.

## Testimonials And Social Proof

Before using proof, confirm:

- Who said it.
- Whether it can be quoted.
- Whether edits preserve meaning.
- Whether disclosure is needed.
- Whether the result is typical or exceptional.

If proof is unavailable, write credibility alternatives:

- Process proof.
- Founder story.
- Behind-the-scenes detail.
- Product walkthrough.
- Educational content.
- Clear explanation of value.

## Email And Outreach

- Be honest about identity and reason for contact.
- Do not mislead in subject lines.
- Include unsubscribe or opt-out guidance when appropriate.
- Do not scrape, spam, or pressure.
- Personalize outreach enough to show relevance.

## Influencer And Affiliate

- Require disclosure for paid, gifted, sponsored, affiliate, or commission-based relationships.
- Do not hide compensation.
- Do not ask creators to make claims they cannot support.
- Provide approved talking points and prohibited claims.

## Paid Ads

- Check platform-specific policies before launch.
- Avoid personal-attribute targeting language in ad copy.
- Do not imply the viewer has a sensitive condition, financial status, or personal problem.
- Use compliant landing pages that match the ad claim.

## Compliance Output

When reviewing an asset, return:

```md
# Compliance Check

STATUS: Approved / Needs Revision / Blocked
RISK LEVEL: Low / Medium / High
ISSUES:
- [Issue]
FIXES:
- [Revision]
APPROVED CLAIMS:
- [Claim]
DO NOT SAY:
- [Restricted wording]
```
