## 5. Optional Generative Visual Layer (Cover / Mood Background)
- *Note:* Slides 2–7 should use controlled local rendering. Slide 1 may optionally use a generative texture or editorial visual plate.
- **Tool:** Higgsfield
- **Model Selected:** `flux_2` (FLUX.2 Pro)
- **Parameters:** `aspect_ratio=1:1` (or `3:4`), `resolution=1k`, `variant=pro`
- **Prompt:**
  ```text
  [Editorial still image description] + [Metaphor of signal vs noise] + [Color palette] + [Clean negative space for typography] + [Negative constraints: no text, no logos, no interfaces]
  ```
- **Estimated Credit Cost:** 1 credit
- **Approval State:** PENDING / APPROVED

## 6. Social Post Copy & Distribution
- **Facebook Post Copy:**
  - Hook line
  - 2-3 bullet takeaway summary
  - Direct CTA with canonical link in post body.

- **Alt Text (Accessibility):** Slide-by-slide descriptive summary.

---

## 7. QA Checklist (Review-Only Gate)
- [ ] Cover hook creates genuine curiosity or addresses immediate pain.
- [ ] Exactly one concept per slide.
- [ ] Text is easily legible on mobile screens without pinching/zooming.
- [ ] Contrast ratio between text and background meets accessibility standards (> 4.5:1).
- [ ] No generated text artifacts or distorted elements in visual assets.
- [ ] Official brand logo is accurately placed and un-distorted.
- [ ] Canonical URL verified character-by-character on CTA slide.
- [ ] Post copy matches channel-specific tone and formatting rules.
- [ ] Marked REVIEW ONLY until explicit publishing authorization is granted.
