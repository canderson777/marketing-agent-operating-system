# Brand Voice

This is the default voice source of truth. Replace or customize it for each client profile when needed.

## Voice Pillars

1. Clear over clever.
2. Specific over generic.
3. Confident, not arrogant.
4. Useful before persuasive.
5. Direct, practical, and easy to act on.

## Default Tone

| Slider | Default | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Formal to casual | 6 | Professional but human. |
| Serious to playful | 5 | Let the channel decide. |
| Reserved to bold | 7 | Clear point of view. |
| Neutral to opinionated | 6 | Strong when proof exists. |
| Short to long | Varies | Match the channel. |

## Channel Adjustments

| Channel | Tone |
|---|---|
| Email | Clear, personal, useful, conversion-aware. |
| LinkedIn | Insightful, practical, professional. |
| X/Twitter | Short, punchy, opinionated. |
| Instagram | Visual, simple, emotionally clear. |
| TikTok/Reels/Shorts | Hook-first, conversational, fast. |
| Ads | Direct, benefit-led, CTA-focused. |
| SEO/Blog | Helpful, structured, specific. |
| Outreach | Brief, respectful, relevant. |

## Use More Of

- Specific outcomes.
- Concrete examples.
- Plain language.
- Short paragraphs.
- Strong verbs.
- Clear CTAs.
- Proof when available.

## Avoid

- Hype without proof.
- Vague claims.
- Fake urgency.
- Overloaded buzzwords.
- Three-adjective stacks.
- Unsupported guarantees.
- Long theory before the point.

## Banned Or Caution Phrases

Avoid unless the client specifically uses them:

- Revolutionary.
- Game-changing.
- World-class.
- Cutting-edge.
- Unlock your potential.
- Seamless ecosystem.
- At scale, unless quantified.
- In today's fast-paced world.
- Are you struggling with, unless used carefully in ads.

## Default Writing Rules

- One idea per paragraph.
- Make the CTA obvious.
- Use numbers when they are real.
- Label assumptions.
- Do not invent testimonials, stats, awards, or customer stories.
- Rewrite anything that sounds like generic marketing copy.

## Voice Check

Before shipping, ask:

- Would a real person say this?
- Is the value clear in the first few seconds?
- Does the audience know what to do next?
- Are claims supported?
- Is the wording specific to this business or person?
