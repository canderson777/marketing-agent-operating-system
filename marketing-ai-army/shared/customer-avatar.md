# Customer Avatar

Use this as the default audience model when a completed profile does not provide enough detail. Replace it with client-specific details whenever possible.

## Core Avatar Fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Segment | The specific group most likely to act now. |
| Job to be done | What they are trying to accomplish. |
| Pain | What is frustrating, expensive, slow, confusing, or risky. |
| Desired outcome | What better situation they want. |
| Trigger | Why they would act now. |
| Objection | Why they might hesitate. |
| Proof needed | What would make them believe. |
| CTA fit | The next step they are willing to take. |

## Business Audience Defaults

If the profile is a business and the audience is vague, assume:

- The audience wants a practical result, not more theory.
- They compare cost, time, trust, and proof.
- They respond to clear examples and simple next steps.
- They need a reason to act now.

## Personal Brand Or Creator Defaults

If the profile is a personal brand and the audience is vague, assume:

- The audience follows for personality plus useful perspective.
- They need repeated exposure before buying.
- Trust grows through consistency, proof, vulnerability, and clarity.
- Content should balance education, story, and conversion.

## Avatar Output

When asked to define an avatar, return:

- Primary audience.
- Secondary audience.
- Pain points.
- Desired outcomes.
- Buying or following triggers.
- Objections.
- Best channels.
- Best messages.
- CTA recommendations.
