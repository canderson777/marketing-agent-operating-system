# Social Campaign Brief Workflow

**Purpose:** Turn one brand offer or growth goal into a focused, channel-aware creative brief without jumping into production.

## Required inputs

- Brand and source-of-truth files
- Person / Pain / Promise
- Campaign goal and primary metric
- Audience and objection
- Offer and one primary CTA
- Channels and format requirements
- Source material
- Visual constraints and approved assets
- Approval boundary
- Budget or generation-credit limit, if applicable

## Procedure

1. Read the brand context, Three P's, current priorities, and relevant channel workflows.
2. State the single business objective and primary metric.
3. Choose one audience segment and one pain to address.
4. Write one promise grounded in the brand files.
5. Select one CTA. Separate conversion creative from awareness creative.
6. Assign channel roles. Do not mirror one asset everywhere automatically.
7. Define a small pilot before proposing a large content calendar.
8. Separate verified facts, assumptions, recommendations, and `NEEDS OWNER INPUT` items.
9. Specify review and approval gates before any external tool call or publishing action.
10. Define the tracker fields needed to compare results.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent customer claims, metrics, testimonials, or audience research.
- Do not start with a tool or visual effect. Start with the message and use case.
- Do not send, publish, spend, or modify an account from this workflow.
- If an external generator has usage cost, show model, prompt, parameters, and estimated cost before generation and wait for operator approval.
- Keep brand-specific facts in the brand folder. Keep this workflow reusable.

## Handoff

Pass the locked brief to the Reel, carousel, social-card, or channel workflow. The next workflow must retain the same audience, promise, CTA, approval state, and metric.
