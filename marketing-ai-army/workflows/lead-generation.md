# Lead Generation Workflow

Use this when the goal is more leads, booked calls, signups, demos, trials, inquiries, or qualified conversations.

## Goal
Create a practical lead generation system with a clear offer, traffic source, capture path, and follow-up.

## Required Inputs
- Client profile.
- Target audience.
- Lead offer or main offer.
- CTA.
- Timeline.
- Budget.
- Existing assets.
- Current lead source metrics.

## Agents Used
- Master Marketing Agent.
- Direct Outreach Agent for fast conversations.
- Content Marketing Agent for lead magnets and landing pages.
- Email Marketing Agent for nurture.
- Paid Ads Agent when budget exists.
- SEO/AEO and Social Media for organic lead flow.

## Steps

1. **Define Lead**
   - What counts as a lead?
   - What is the qualification standard?
   - What is the next action after capture?

2. **Choose Lead Path**
   - Direct outreach.
   - Lead magnet.
   - Paid ad to landing page.
   - Organic content to signup.
   - Webinar or event.
   - Partnership referral.

3. **Create Capture Asset**
   - Landing page, booking page, form, DM keyword, or lead magnet.

4. **Create Follow-Up**
   - Email sequence, sales script, or reply handling.

5. **Launch And Track**
   - Define daily or weekly activity targets.
   - Track conversion metrics.

```md
[Path]

## Assets Needed
- [Asset]

|---|---|---|

[Targets]

[Metrics]

[Action]
```

## Acceptance Criteria
- Lead definition is specific.
- CTA is low-friction.
- Follow-up exists.
- Metrics include conversion rate and cost or effort per lead.
