# Workflow: Product Launch

Use this for a new product, service, course, community, creator offer, or major relaunch.

## Goal

Launch a new offer with a structured pre-launch, launch window, and evergreen transition instead of treating launch day as a single event.

## Trigger

`../master-agent.md` receives the product, launch date, audience, positioning, target, and budget.

## Phase 0 - Strategy Lock

- Lock positioning, hero hook, price, guarantee, bonuses, and CTA.
- Update `../shared/offer-library.md` with the launch offer.
- Update the audience model if the launch targets a new segment.
- Flag compliance-sensitive claims early.
- Brief the required channel sub-masters.

## Phase 1 - Pre-Launch Content

- Content creates founder story, product education, proof, and lead magnets.
- SEO/AEO creates product, comparison, FAQ, and alternatives content where relevant.
- Email builds waitlist or countdown flows.
- Compliance reviews launch claims before promotion accelerates.

## Phase 2 - Pre-Launch Buzz

- Social runs teaser, why-now, proof, and countdown arcs.
- Email sends announcement and waitlist broadcasts.
- Partnerships, influencers, affiliates, and events get their launch kits and timing.
- Paid assets are prepared and warm audiences are prepped when appropriate.
- Direct outreach warms selected high-value prospects.

## Phase 3 - Launch Day

- Email, social, paid, affiliates, influencers, partners, community, and events all activate on the approved plan.
- The master agent collects same-day numbers and coordinates changes.

## Phase 4 - Launch Window

- Email handles proof, objections, and FAQ.
- Social amplifies customer voice, founder voice, and urgency.
- Paid scales winners and refreshes creative.
- Community and event follow-through continue the conversation.

## Phase 5 - Close Day

- Morning, mid-day, and last-call assets ship where appropriate.
- Urgency stays truthful.
- Cart-abandonment or late-decision flows run if relevant.

## Phase 6 - Transition To Evergreen

- Remove launch-only urgency.
- Move the strongest launch assets into evergreen funnels.
- Keep winning creatives and pages alive where useful.
- Turn launch learnings into permanent onboarding, content, or offer improvements.

## Phase 7 - Post-Mortem

- Compare revenue or conversions against target.
- Review per-channel contribution and attribution.
- Capture learnings, winning creative, objections, and proof.
- Decide whether to scale, iterate, or pivot.

## Output Location

`outputs/campaigns/[YYYY-MM-DD_launch-product-slug]/`

## Kill Criteria

- If pre-launch demand is materially below target, call a strategy huddle before launch day.
- If launch-day results are well below plan, adjust messaging, timing, or offer structure immediately.
